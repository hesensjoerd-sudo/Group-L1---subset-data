How to use the diagram
  1. Step 1: Click a category to show its connections. For the first column, connected pathways continue from column two to column three.
  2. Step 2: Click a connection line or “Paper IDs” to see the studies behind that connection.
  3. Step 3: Click the selected category again or select “Show all connections” to reset the diagram.

A paper may be assigned to multiple categories. N represents the number of unique papers in each category.

Select a category

Select a category to highlight its connections. Selecting a category in the first column also shows the outgoing lines from its connected categories in the second column.
